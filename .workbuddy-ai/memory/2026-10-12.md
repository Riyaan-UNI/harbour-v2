## Brand assets — recoloured to the palette

The user asked for the logo colours to match the design file, and the tagline too. Two
decisions were put to them and answered: **snap the two off-palette values to the
nearest tokens**, and **use spec §9's headline as the tagline**.

- `#B9DFE1` → `#BFE5E2` (light `--aurora-1`) — the mid wave, in all three files.
- `#55756B` → `#496662` (light `--text-subtle`) — the tagline, in the logo.
- `#0B3935`, `#F1C96F`, `#F7F3E9` already matched light `accent-fill`, `gold-fill` and
  `bg` exactly, so they were left alone.

**All 13 hex values across the three files are now exact spec §3 values** (verified by
script; zero off-palette). Each file carries a comment naming the token behind each hex,
so the mapping is auditable. The `<title>` was updated too — it still said "Find your
footing".

**The tagline measurement mattered.** I had told the user the canvas would need widening
to ~560px. Measuring the real text in Chromium showed the new line at weight 500 /
tracking 0.4 is **169.8px** wide against the old strapline's **167.9px** — so the lockup's
footprint is unchanged and `viewBox` stays 510×128. The weight and tracking had to come
down from 700 / 3.2 because that treatment is for a short uppercase strapline and reads
as broken on sentence case.

**Two findings to carry forward, neither acted on (scope was "colours only"):**

1. **These are light-background lockups.** The wordmark is `#0B3935` on a page background
   of `#061B1A` in the dark theme — effectively invisible. The mark survives because its
   cream strokes sit inside the circle, but its outer edge vanishes. Dark theme is the
   default, so this needs a decision: a dark variant, or `currentColor`/tokens.
2. **`harbour-logo.svg` has hard `width="510" height="128"` attributes**, so it does not
   scale to a container — dropping it into the nav gives a 510px-wide logo. The mark and
   favicon have no width/height and scale freely. Removing the two attributes fixes it.
